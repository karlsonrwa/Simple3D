## The fix

- `stepbuilder/settings.py`: `without_launch_overrides(values, launched, loaded)` - every setting that differs between what was loaded and the window's values right after the flags, and that is still as the flags left it, goes back to what was loaded; a setting changed in the window since stays; the window's place and the paths it was pointed at are never a flag's (`_NOT_FROM_FLAGS`) (R6-4).
- `stepbuilder/__main__.py`: `app.note_launch_values()` after the flags, before the board's paths are filled in (R6-4).
- `stepbuilder/gui.py`: `note_launch_values` and `_gui_values`, and `_save_config` through `without_launch_overrides` (R6-4); `set_theme` takes one of `BOARD_THEMES` in any case and answers whether it did, and a name it has no theme for keeps the colour and says so in the log, naming the themes and what the command line's `r,g,b` and `#rrggbb` are for (R6n-6); `_refresh_layers` during a build only notes that it is wanted and `_set_busy(False)` runs it, `_update_silk_row` keeps out of a build (R6-11); `_reset_layer_colors` resets the kinds painted AND with a swatch, and its "every colour" log line compares with the swatches (R6-15); `_on_close` asks, while a build runs, whether to close and stop it, and keeps the window on Cancel (R6-14); `on_cancel`'s log line says what a kill leaves.
- `stepbuilder/worker_bridge.py`: one `multiprocessing.Queue` per build, made by `start`, dropped by `cancel` and `close` (`_drop_queue`); `drain_once` with no queue only looks for a death (R6-12, R6n-5).
- `stepbuilder/stepdoc.py`: `StepDocument.write` writes `<name>.step.part` and renames it into place with `os.replace` once the writer reports done; on any error, an interrupt included, the part is taken away and the error goes on (R6-14).
- `stepbuilder/winplace.py`: `monitor_areas` - each monitor's work area, the screen less its taskbar, from `EnumDisplayMonitors` and `GetMonitorInfoW`'s `rcWork`, the virtual screen when they cannot be listed; `geometry_is_reachable(areas, ...)` - the title strip, the top 40 px of the frame and at least 120 px of them wide, on ONE work area (R6-6); `primary_work_area` (`SPI_GETWORKAREA`) and `center_on_primary` within it, no taller than the work area less `FRAME_ALLOWANCE` (48, the measured 47 rounded up) (R6-7).
- Tests: `tests/test_window_rules.py`, new, nine sections - the real window built and asked, no screen, no clipboard, no child process, so these rules are proved where the window suites cannot run; test_launcher's stand-in window gained `note_launch_values` and a check of what it noted, and its crash box is a recorder with a final check that the launcher never fell into its handler; test_geom's centring checks take the primary's work area, [6] the monitor lists, [9] is new (a 1366x768 work area, Generate inside the client); test_gui's three vacuous checks give way to pointers at test_window_rules [8] (R6-9). Rows 568 -> 597: 29 new, the three on winplace's old rule re-pointed at the new one, the three on the suite count at 32, two old rows given the new suite that answers in session 0, and round 127's row on the Russian singular count re-pointed at the form the docs use now (below).
