## The fix

- `stepbuilder/board.py`: `_board_face(contour, z, what)` - the wire (its error prefixed with *what*), MakeFace, BRepCheck (a rejected wire means "crosses itself", anything else "is not a valid face"), then the area against 1 um2 (after BRepCheck: the bow-tie would read as "no area"). Used for the outline, `_zone_solid`, each zone once at the head of `layer_solids`, and each cutout once in `_checked_cutouts` (numbered by its place in pcb.edges, as the doubled-track warnings are, and placed by where it begins: `Cutout 3 near (2, 6)`).
- `stepbuilder/contour.py`: a segment of no length and an arc of no sweep raise a StepBuilderError with their number and place; `_face_from_wires` says "outline makes no flat face (OpenCASCADE: <error>)".
- `stepbuilder/intermediate.py`: `validate()` checks every component placed from a model for `COMPONENT_KEYS` (x, y, angle, is_mirrored) and `MAPPING_KEYS` (rotation_x/y/z, offset_x/y/z), numbers not bools, is_mirrored only for being there; a component with no model is left to the build, which skips it with its warning.
- `stepbuilder/models.py` (R3-4): a second cache keyed on the resolved file (`os.path.normcase`), the part named after the file on disk.
- `stepbuilder/core.py` (R3-8): `warning: zone X's stackup 'S' has no layers - the zone is left out of the board`.
- Tests: test_zones [3] against the zones' union (STTIFFENER1 and FLEX1 share 0.14 x 16.34 mm), within 1e-6; test_layers [5] exactly one solid.
