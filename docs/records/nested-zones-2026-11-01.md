# Record: a zone drawn inside another (01.11.2026, round 127)

R3-6 and R3-7 of the 23.10.2026 review: the inspect build ("Not stitched") held the area of a zone nested in another twice, and a thin zone inside a thick one would have been built thick. Parked in round 125 for the owner, because Cadence's rigid-flex book says zones cannot be nested and so seemed to give such a zone no meaning (`docs/records/body-contours-2026-11-01.md`). Unparked the same day: the owner sent Cadence's own pages on nested zones and asked for the fix at once («Да, сейчас»). Scripts and outputs in `build/fix-2026-10-31/r127/`.

## What Cadence says

- Cadence blog, BoardSurfers "The Power of Nesting Stackup Zones", 18.04.2023, tagged 22.1: <https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/nesting-stackup-zones-in-allegro-pcb-editor>. A zone may lie inside another; zones that cross are still trimmed, the new one to the old one's boundary. A nested zone is for an area that needs another soldermask or plating, given in its own stackup; the inner zone may differ from the one around it in the mask layers, the etch layers must match. Its figure draws "Zone 2 (Nested)" inside a whole Zone 1, with no void.
- Cadence community forum, "How to create and edit nested zones for Rigid-Flex PCB", 28.02.2024: <https://community.cadence.com/cadence_technology_forums/pcb-design/f/allegro-x-pcb-editor/58982/how-to-create-and-edit-nested-zones-for-rigid-flex-pcb>. From release 22.1 QIR2 one zone can be defined inside another (Setup > Zones > Create, twice); before it, a zone surrounded by another was not recommended.
- Installed on this machine: "Migration Guide for Allegro X Platform Products", Product Version 24.1, September 2024, chapter "Allegro X Platform Core Back-End Products", section Database Format: a design with nested zones saves back to no release older than 22.1 Hotfix 003 (QIR2). `file:///D:/Cadence/SPB_24.1/doc/PCBmigration/Allegro_X_Platform_Core_Back-End_Products.html`. The 25.1 migration guide no longer mentions zones.
- Out of date: "Working with Rigid-Flex Designs", Product Version 25.1, September 2025, page "Creating Multi-Stackup Zones in the Design Drawing", still lists "Zones cannot be overlapped or nested" among its zone rules. The round-125 search covered only the 25.1 help (the text index of `cadence-docs` is built from it), not the 24.1 help also installed, nor Cadence's site.
- Allegro's own answers on the owner's boards (`build/fix-2026-10-31/nested/probe_zone_at_point.il`): `axlZoneAccess('point)` names the inner zone at all 215 grid points lying in two zones (70 on flex-b2, 91 and 54 on flex2-a0), and the three parts whose origin lies there (X1 on flex-b2, XP1 and X1 on flex2-a0) are in the inner zone (`symbol->zone`).

So the meaning is Cadence's: inside the inner zone the board is the inner zone's stack, and the zone around it holds the rest.

## Measured before the fix

- How the zones of the eight review exports lie against each other (`zone_pairs.py`, OCC faces of the contours, arcs and all): three pairs nested, the inner zone wholly inside, 0.000000 mm2 outside - flex-b2's SOLDERMASK1 (61.607593 mm2) inside STIFFENER2 (182.08), flex2-a0's LDI (21.620398) inside STIFF_TOP_500 (32.392699) and LDI2 (21.341847) inside STIFF_TOP_500_2 (128.679237). The only other pair sharing any area, flex2-a0's STIFF_TOP_500_2 and FLEX, shares 1e-6 mm2, 5e-9 of the smaller: they touch. Cadence's demo (7 zones), flex3-a0 (3) and the four plain boards have none.
- The stacks differ: SOLDERMASK1 has soldermask where STIFFENER2 has a 2 mm stiffener, adhesive and coverlay; LDI and LDI2 have a bottom soldermask where their outer zones have adhesive and coverlay. What each stack puts inside the inner zone (`nested_material.py`, `board._layer_region` and `layer_solids`, the body's own functions): the outer stack's material there is only the shared core (TOP, the dielectric, BOTTOM) - and on flex2-a0's LDI also the stiffener, adhesive and top coverlay, which LDI's stack has too - so the two stacks fused inside the inner zone are exactly the inner one: 16.326 mm3 both ways in SOLDERMASK1, 4.5885 in LDI2, 17.0801 in LDI. What the inspect build counts twice there: 13.2456, 4.055 and 16.5396 mm3 (the cutouts not taken out).
- On a synthetic board (`tests/test_nested.py`, red on HEAD: 21 of 27 checks FAIL): the fixture's S2 (x 0..16, y 0..11.38, the 2.315 mm STIFFENER2 stack) given a zone W (x 2..14, y 3..8) on the 0.365 mm FLEX stack - the body 649.286 mm3 where W's own stack gives 530.786; Not stitched 669.686; at (8, 5) the surface, a part placed in W, the pads' lookup without a surface, the mask window, the drawn openings, the legend, a bend's axis and its crooked-bend note all answered S2.

## The fix

- `stepbuilder/zones.py` (new): `nest(zones, log)` - a zone whose area lies inside another to NEST_SHARE (1e-6 of its own area) and is the smaller is given to it under "nested" (as contours), once, in `core._prepare_stackups`, with a log line "Zone W lies inside zone S2: the board there is W's stack (FLEX), not both"; `own_face(contour, nested, z)` - a zone's own region, its contour less the zones nested in it, as separate tools of one Cut; `in_nested(point, polygons)` - a point inside a nested zone, not on its edge, is in that zone and not in this one.
- Every question about a point or an area asks a zone's own region: `board._layer_region` (the body, Not stitched, Solid colored layers, and so `layer_holds_material` - a zone's levels and the datum), `board._zone_solid` (a file without stackup layers), `surface.Surface._in_zone` and `_faces`, `pads._Zones.at` and `mask_at` (`holds`), the drawn openings' homes and the region they are clipped to (`build_exposed`), `legend.clip_silk_to_zones`, the fold plan's `_stack_at`, `crooked.crooked_parts`, `core._note_bends_over_empty_layers`.
- Zones that cross - which Allegro does not draw - are not nested: both still count where they overlap, as before (test_surface [1]).
