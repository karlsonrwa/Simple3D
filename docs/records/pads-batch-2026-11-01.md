## The fix

- `stepbuilder/pads.py`: `on_board` asks, once per grid cell, every cutout whose box holds the point and none of whose edges passes through the cell (R4-1); masks are known from format_version 11 on, or when the library has any (R4-5); `build_exposed` counts per polygon what is left of it inside the zone, by a Common of the polygon's own face with the region (`build_silkscreen(made=)` hands back each polygon's face, `_left_of(face, region)`), and says the ones left with nothing apart (R4n-12); `figure_face` notes a figure other than a rectangle or a square whose box stands in for a missing outline (R4-8); `pin_sides` reads a through padstack's span as a range of its layers (R4-6).
- `stepbuilder/core.py`: four heights - a flat legend h, the windows 2h, the copper 3h, the drawn openings' parts 4h (R4-3); the copper under drawn openings asks the stackups as exported which zones have a mask, the laminate keeps asking the model (R4n-13).
- `skill/s3d_pads.il`: "text" in the copper sweep's filter (R4-4); a warning when the via selection fails (R4n-15); a warning for a pad figure of more than one path (R4n-16).
- R4-10: `tools/probes/probe_pads_masks.il` (every pin's and via's copper and mask polygons as Allegro reports them, both sides), `tests/fixtures/pads_masks.json` (86 pins and vias of Cadence's demo, its v12 export's library, `build/fix-2026-10-31/r126/make_masks_fixture.py`) and test_pads [20]: box within the demo's 1 um grid, area within the boundary moved by one step; the unused board of [10] replaced by the two refusals.
- Found after the 4090's comparison of the final code (01.11.2026, 14:37) and fixed in the same round: the first version asked the group's Common for each polygon's history (`IsDeleted`, `Modified`) and, with an empty history, looked the face up in the result. Now each polygon is asked by a Common of its own; test_pads [15] has two overlapping polygons across the board's edge (red on the first version: 1 built of 3, both said to have nothing on the board) and asks the rule alone (half in, touching, away).
- Found by the same 4090 run (14:26): test_surface still pinned the old heights (a window h, the copper 2h, a drawn opening 3h) - 8 FAIL, and 15 mutation rows of its suite UNPROVEN on the red copy.
