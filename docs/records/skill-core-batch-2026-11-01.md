## Measured before the fix

- **R1-3, units.** No call asked the design units; the reference pages of `axlStepGet` and `axlXSectionGet` give offsets and thicknesses in design units. `tools/probes/probe_units.il` on a copy of circle-A0: `axlDBGetDesignUnits()` answered `("millimeters" 4 10000)`; turned to mils with `axlDBChangeDesignUnits( "mils" 2 )` it answered `("mils" 2 100)`.
- **R1-4, a mirrored pin's drill offset.** `tools/probes/probe_drill_offset.il`: every drilled padstack of copies of Cadence's demo and flex3-a0 given `drillOffset (0.375 0.25)`, each drilled component pin's own drill read from Allegro (the centre of `axlPolyFromDrillHole`'s box) against `s3dDrillXY`'s: the 235 pins not mirrored, at 0, 90, 180 and 270 degrees, equal; the 4 mirrored ones - the demo's J7 at 90 degrees, flex3-a0's two at 180 - 0.750 mm off, twice the offset's x, where flipping x before the turn lands on Allegro's drill exactly. The demo's 28 mirrored pins at 0 degrees could not be asked: `axlPadstackEdit` answered nil for their two padstacks. A first version of the probe moved a part with `axlTransformObject` and read its pins after: they kept rotation 0 and mirror nil while Allegro's drill had moved, so the placements are the board's own.
- **R1-5, a part in no zone.** The export wrote `"zone": null` with no line; the STEP tool then stood the part on the board's thickest face. On the fixture of test_surface a part with no zone at (30, 5), where no zone is: no line in the log (test_surface [2], red on HEAD's core).
- **R1-6, pcb.thickness.** Its soldermask_top and soldermask_bottom are by position since round 76 - everything outside the outer conductors. A flex stack (coverlay, no mask) built Solid with *Do not include soldermask*: 0.215 mm; the layer build of the same file 0.365 (test_nomask [7], red on HEAD).
- **R1-7, axlStepGet's argument.** `tools/probes/probe_stepget.il` on a copy of my_test_board-a0, part SB1: the instance and its symdef answered the footprint's model; a device binding made as well (`axlStepSet` on the compdef, `cap_D8x10mm.stp`, offset_z 1.5 - it answered nil until the STEP path was set), the instance still answered the footprint's; the footprint's deleted, the device's alone, the instance answered **nil** - the part would be left out as having no model - while `axlStepMappedInstance` answered t.
- **R1-8, a slot's first arc.** Written with ccw "false" though the contour enters it at alpha: walked in order (contour_points) a 0.6 x 2.0 slot measured 0.840 mm2 for 1.123, the two half circles cancelling (the review; flex-b2's golden slot 1.680 for 1.963). The solid never showed it - OpenCASCADE joins edges in any order.
- **R1n-10, polarity.** `s3dLayerIsNegative` looked for a key unbroken in the upper-cased function and name; the STEP tool reads a soldermask in letters and digits only (`stackup._is_soldermask`), so a function spelled SOLDER_MASK was a mask to one side and not negative to the other.
- **R1n-11, check 6.** `axlUIWPrint( nil msg )`: the message was taken for a level and a third argument asked; HEAD's `check_formats` saw one of `axlUIWPrint( nil msg )` and `axlUIWPrint( nil 'warn msg )`.
- **R1n-12, test_emit [4].** Its line test saw a value glued to `"\""` alone; `strcat( "\"key\": \"" value "\",\n" )` passed it.
- **R1n-13, silent refusals.** Six `errset`s the review named in s3d_stackup.il, two more of the same kind there (`s3dStackupJson`, `s3dStackupsJson`) and `s3dContourJson`'s walk swallowed a refusal: a plain board taken for one with no stackups, a layer's thickness 0, a zone or its outline gone, an outline cut short - and nothing said.
- **R1n-15, parts without a model.** Named by the menu command only; a console run or `tools/skill_export.py` left them out without a word.

## The fix

- `skill/s3d_export.il`: `makeVariant3dIntermediates` refuses a design in any unit but millimeters before anything is written, naming its unit (R1-3), and names the parts without a STEP model itself, once (`s3dCheckMfrPn`; the menu's own line removed, it would be said twice) (R1n-15); `symbolReturn3DElements` names a part whose origin lies in no zone on a board with zones, once per export, with its place (R1-5), takes its mapping from `s3dStepMapping` and names a part bound to two different models (R1-7).
- `skill/s3d_props.il`: `s3dStepMapping` - the footprint's binding, else the device's; `s3dHasStepModel` and `s3dCheckMfrPn` ask it too (R1-7).
- `skill/s3d_geometry.il`: `s3dDrillXY` flips a mirrored pin's offset x before the turn (R1-4); `makeSlotAt`'s first half circle entered at alpha, ccw "true" (R1-8); `s3dContourJson` names an outline it cannot read whole, with its box (R1n-13).
- `skill/s3d_stackup.il`: `s3dLayerIsNegative` reads the function and the name each on its own, in letters and digits only (`s3dLettersAndDigits`) (R1n-10); eight `errset`s print Allegro's message and say what the refusal costs (R1n-13).
- `stepbuilder/core.py`: `thickness_without_soldermask` - with *Do not include soldermask* a plain board loses the board stackup's SOLDERMASK layers, as `drop_soldermask` takes them off the layers, not everything outside the conductors (R1-6); a part whose zone is unknown and whose origin no zone holds is named with the face it is put on (R1-5).
- `tools/skill_checks.py`: one argument after `axlUIWPrint`'s form is the format (R1n-11). `tests/test_emit.py` [4]: strcat's own arguments are asked (R1n-12).
- `simple3d.il`: the menu's no-model line gone (the export says it now).
